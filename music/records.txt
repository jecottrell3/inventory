* DESCRIPTION *

This is RECORDS.TXT, a Companion To and Description Of RECORDS.CSV. It
originally started as my Handy Note Book listing my LPs. Each was
Numbered, Labeled, Timed, and Entered into the Book. And some point,
LPs stopped being sold and the book languished.

Later, I traded a lot of Grateful Dead cassette tapes, made some of my
own, and bought some. VCRs became common, and I taped a lot of Grateful
Dead Hour broadcasts over Random Movies.

Finally came CDs, and then Writable CDs.

* LEGEND *

The RECORDS.CSV should be imported into a Spreadsheet.
Periodically, I do this, but the CSV file I Edit Directly,
which is why the Initial Fields are Fixed Width.

The Fields and their Meanings are as follows:

Issued,Num,MT,N,Type,Artist,Album,Comments

+ Issued	Release Date
+ Num		Sequence Number
+ MT		Media Type
+ N		Pieces
+ Type		Genre
+ Group		Artist
+ Album		Title
+ Comments	Optional, Flags

Where possible, the fields are Fixed Width.

+ In Dates, a 00 means Haven't Looked, and 99 means Can't Find.

+ MT:
NX=Next; CD=Bought; CC=Copied; KP=Keep; LP=Vinyl;
SL=VL=Sell; TP=Cassette; TC=Tape Copy; TH=Tape Half; XX=Gone;

Future MTs may be Related, as in GP=Glenda

* END *
